# SubRadar Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-17_

SubRadar is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains, plainly, what that means for your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** SubRadar does not have a server, does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not require an account. Every subscription you track — name, amount, billing date, notes — is stored only in a local database on your phone, using Apple's / Google's standard on-device storage.

## In-app purchases

The one-time "Pro" unlock is processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. SubRadar never sees your payment details — Apple/Google share only a purchase confirmation, which we use to store a `true`/`false` flag locally on your device.

## Notifications

Renewal reminders are scheduled locally on your device using your operating system's notification system. No reminder data is sent anywhere.

## Sharing data with third parties

We don't, because we never receive it in the first place. There are no third-party SDKs in this app that collect data.

## CSV export/import

When you export your data to CSV, the file is created locally and shared using your device's native share sheet — it goes wherever you choose to send it (e.g., Files, email, AirDrop), and only there. Importing a CSV reads the file you pick and never leaves your device.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored subscription data immediately, since nothing exists anywhere else.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (e.g., if a future version adds an optional cloud backup feature), we'll update this document and the version history in this repository before that feature ships.
