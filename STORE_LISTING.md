## App name

SubRadar

## Subtitle / short description (Play: 80 chars max)

Track subscriptions & bills. 100% offline — your data never leaves your phone.

## Description

Stop getting surprised by renewals. SubRadar tracks every subscription and recurring bill in one place, reminds you before they charge, and shows you exactly where your money goes each month.

**Why SubRadar**
- 🔒 Private by design — everything is stored on your device. No account, no server, no data collection. (Full privacy policy: see below.)
- 🔔 Renewal reminders — get a local notification a couple of days before each subscription bills you.
- 👥 Split subscriptions fairly — track your actual share of family plans and shared accounts, not just the sticker price.
- 📊 Spending breakdown — see what you're really spending per category, per month and per year.
- 📤 Your data, portable — export and import CSV any time. No lock-in.

**Free plan**
Track up to 5 subscriptions for free, forever.

**SubRadar Pro** (one-time purchase, no subscription)
- Unlimited subscriptions
- CSV import & export
- Spending stats & category breakdown
